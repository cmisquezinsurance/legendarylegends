Legends

A battle game by Jaxton

Pick your skin

  • Buddies: every player has a little flying robot buddy that follows them, shoots lasers at enemies and slowly heals them when there is no fight. Your buddy is Zappy, a thundercloud that shoots lightning.
  • Squads of four. You and 3 buddies against 24 other squads. That is 100 players on a giant map! Last squad standing wins.
  • Chests hold super powers, weapons (bow and arrows, hammers, the Storm Blaster, the Laser Rifle and the Mega Laser Beam), healing potions, invisible potions and coins. There are 2 godly chests each game with the best power, hidden somewhere on the map. It only glows when you get close.
  • 10 powers: rock, fire, water, telepathy, kryptonite, air, earth, slime, laser eyes and godly power.
  • The storm: stay out of the red storm clouds or you lose health. Red tornados spin around in the storm and fling you around, and red lightning strikes inside the storm. The storm destroys everything it covers: houses, trees, rocks, chests and even the castle. When 3 squads are left, the storm gets faster and stronger and more tornados show up.
  • Villages have two-floor homes, stores and schools with villagers walking around. There are big mansions and 3-floor hotels too. Walk up to a door and press OPEN to go inside and find chests. The castle tower is 7 floors tall.
  • Mythic chests (orange) hold the Mythic Bow and top powers. Look in mansions, hotels and the castle tower.
  • If you get knocked out, a squadmate can revive you. Stand next to a knocked teammate and hold REVIVE.
  • A galaxy dragon sits on top of the castle tower and shoots glowing galaxy balls at everyone. Wherever they land, everything catches on fire: the ground, trees, houses and fields burn down. Sometimes it flies around the castle. Slay it for 400 coins and mythic loot.
  • Farms: fields of wheat, corn, pumpkins and cabbage, red barns with chests inside, and cows, pigs, sheep, chickens and horses.
  • Bosses roam the map and attack every team. The King Boss rules the castle. He is the strongest of all, but beat him for huge loot.
  • Spend coins at the Thunder Storm store. Win to unlock new skins and earn a godly chest that waits next to you at the start of your next game.

Phone: joystick on the left to move, swipe the right side to turn and aim like Fortnite, tap Power or Weapon to switch, FIRE to attack, JUMP to dodge, BUILD to put up a wall.
Invisible: drink an invisible potion and nobody can see or target you for 10 seconds.
Computer: WASD to move, move the mouse or arrow keys to turn, click to fire, 1 and 2 to switch, O to open doors or revive, Space to jump, Q to build, E potion, I invisible, M map, T store.
